# Yaki Exiles
X4: Foundations mod to start as a Yaki leading an exiled crew.

This add a new gamestart as a Yaki, nothing too fancy. You flew your faction with you crew and you Kurokami, heads up to Getsu Fune.
You have some high-tech items in your inventory, mostly illegal (so don't be scan too soon).
You have your pirate hoard (1,000,000 Cr) and a decent ship (Kurokami) with you to start anew.
You will start with Xenon Research already researched, since you're a Yaki, you lived among Xenon for many decades.

This gamestart is flagged as "Sandbox", and is mostly for Role-Play purpose. It will not modify gameplay or story unwillingly.

"Kendai had a plan.
You saw a death sentence.
So you took your Kurokami,
your credits (a little hoard),
your knowing of Xenon (and how to replicate their weapons),
and the few allies you could still trust (your crew),
then vanished into Getsu Fune with no way back."

(Hard Start) Most factions view you with suspicion, Terrans will shoot you on sight.
Most Factions will have -5 to -10 reputation towards you. Exception the most open minded, and exiled like you (Segaris Pionners).
Only the most open-minded will hear you out-and the Yaki will see you as an outsider (no story plot completed, it's a fresh start).

## Detailed reputation :
- [5] Segaris : Your only allies, exiled by the Terrans long ago, but still submitted to them
- [5] Quettanauts : Some sentient androids, you didn't met them yet, but they already know you, maybe they will be kind (?)
- [0] Neutral because story plot :
    - Court of Curbs
    - Realm of the Trinity
- [0] Alliance of the World : they aim to unite the worlds, so they are obviously neutral at gamestart
- [-5] Yaki : Your former motherland, now they see you like what you are: an exile they don't want to know
- [-5] Riptides : They are already persecuted, and are willing to get any help they could
- [-5] Buccaneers : Some fellow pirates like the Scale Plate Pact, they will not attack you on sight, but maybe rob you
- [-5] Scale Plate : already "passive" toward anyone, but ready to rob them
- [-5] Vigor Syndicate : they don't care who you are if they can scam you some credits
- [-5] Borons : They see the good in anybody, but don't trust anyone too quickly
- [-10] They look at you with suspicion, trying not to be some dumb racists:
    - Godrealm
    - Free Families
    - Fallen Families
    - Holy Order
    - Teladi
    - Argon
    - Antigone
    - Hatikvah
- [-15] Ministry : They simply don't trust anyone worth trading with
- [-20] Zyarth Patriarchy :  They don't like anyone, especially some wanna be cyborg pirate
- [-20] Terran : Your ennemy, unless you convince them otherwise, what did you expect ?
- [-30] Xenon and Kha'ak : Ennemies of every sentient

# Requirements ?
- Craddle of Humanity DLC (no Yaki otherwise)
## Soft Requirement :
- If you have [Yaki Habitation Module](https://www.nexusmods.com/x4foundations/mods/2032) by iFlisk installed, you will automaticcaly unlock Yaki's hab modules.
- If you have my mod [Timelines Xenon F B and H](https://www.nexusmods.com/x4foundations/mods/1709) installed, you will automatically unlock F, B and H  blueprint research once you have captured those (since you already have Xenon Research researched) -> You need Timelines DLC for this one !
