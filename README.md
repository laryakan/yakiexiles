# yakiexiles
X4: Foundations mod to start as a Yaki leading and exiled crew

Kendai had a plan. You saw a death sentence. 
So you took your Kurokami, your credits, and the few allies you could still trust, then vanished into Getsu Fune with no way back.
(Hard Start) Most factions view you with suspicion. Only the most open-minded will hear you out-and the Yaki will see you as an outsider.

This add a new start as a Yaki, nothing too fancy. You flew your faction with you crew and you Kurokami, heads up to Getsu Fune.

Most Faction will have -5 to -10 reputation to you. Exception the most open minded, and exiled like you (Segaris Pionners).

You have some high-tech items in your inventory, mostly illegal (so don't be scan too soon).

You have you pirate hoard and a decent ship with you to start anew.
